About me

A senior field partner, without the agency layers

I started as a field interviewer and supervisor at Ipsos Egypt, then managed fieldwork in Saudi Arabia and spent nearly three years as a project manager at Kantar.

I now offer my fieldwork services directly to international research agencies. You deal with me from brief to delivery, not with layers of account managers. I work in English and Arabic, and I manage field resources in Egypt and Saudi Arabia directly.

How a project runs

Six steps, one point of contact

01 Brief

Send it any time. I reply the same day.

02 Feasibility and quote

Incidence, timing, risks and a costed quote with clear assumptions.

03 Recruitment

Standard qualitative profiles usually take 5 to 10 working days.

04 Fieldwork

Regular progress updates and early warning on any quota or timing risk.

05 Quality control

Checks before, during and after fieldwork.

06 Delivery

Recordings, transcripts (about 2 working days), data and a QC summary.

Quality control

Quality you can see at every stage

Before fieldwork

Screener review, recruiter and interviewer briefing, pilot or soft launch where relevant, incidence risk check.

During fieldwork

Live quota tracker, interview duration checks, GPS evidence where applicable, daily progress updates.

Validation

Re-screening and participant verification, back-checks or call-backs, recording checks, logic review.

Close-out

Rejected-interview log, replacement tracking, quota reconciliation, QC summary at handover.
